I am a researcher in Artificial Intelligence and Optimization, focusing on the design, analysis, and optimization of algorithms, with a strong emphasis on Mathematical Programming and Machine Learning.

I am currently a **Postdoctoral Researcher** at the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the **University of Western Macedonia**, Greece, where I am a member of the **Intelligent Systems and Optimization (ISOpt) Laboratory**.

I received my Integrated Master's degree in Informatics & Telecommunications Engineering from the University of Western Macedonia. Following my undergraduate studies, I pursued my Ph.D. at the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering of the same university, earning my doctoral degree in 2026. My Ph.D. research centered on developing optimization algorithms for black-box problems. Throughout my academic journey and beyond, I have been actively involved as a researcher in numerous scientific and developmental projects.

## Research Interests and Expertise

My research interests encompass diverse fields within Artificial Intelligence and Optimization. I specialize in developing innovative methodologies for **black-box optimization** and **derivative-free optimization**. A key area of my expertise is **adaptive sampling** using **surrogate models**, where I aim to efficiently navigate complex optimization landscapes where the objective function or constraints are unknown or computationally expensive to evaluate.

Beyond optimization theory, I am highly interested in the intersection of AI and practical applications. I possess a strong background in software development (Python, C++, MATLAB), infrastructure management (DevOps, Cloud), and the implementation of digital solutions. I have actively applied my expertise in machine learning and deep learning (including Large Language Models) across various domains.

During my career, I have worked extensively on research projects tackling real-world challenges, such as:
* Developing intelligent solutions for **Smart Cities** (e.g., optimal waste routing).
* Applying object detection and machine learning algorithms for plant disease prediction in **Precision Agriculture**.
* Designing digital services for energy, environment, and cybersecurity within Digital Innovation Hubs.
* Implementing AI algorithms to support the digital transformation of public administration and entrepreneurship.
